Questions and Answers About Us and Our Products

Q: How should you clean and take care of your PandaShop plant?

A: To keep your hyper realistic faux plants looking fresh and vibrant, regularly dust them with a microfiber cloth, feather duster, or soft brush to prevent buildup. For a deeper clean, gently wipe the leaves with a damp cloth or a mild soap solution, and if needed, rinse them under lukewarm water — just be sure they dry completely. Avoid placing them in direct sunlight for prolonged periods, as this can cause fading. To maintain their natural appearance, occasionally adjust and shape the leaves or branches, especially after unpacking or moving them. If storing them, keep them in a cool, dry place, ensuring they aren’t crushed or bent. With these simple steps, your faux plants will stay as lifelike and beautiful as the day you got them.

On Blooming Artificial's youtube channel, you can see a more detailed video of cleaning methods.

Q: Do your faux plants come with pots?

A: Most of our plants come in stylish pots, but some are sold as stems or with basic planters for easy customization. Product details specify what's included.

Q: Can I use these plants outdoors?

A: While some of our faux plants are designed for outdoor use, others are best kept indoors to prevent fading. Check the product description for outdoor suitability.

Q: Are your faux plants really lifelike?
